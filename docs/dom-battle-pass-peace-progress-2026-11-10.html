READ THE RULES FIRST

  1. To begin the Battle Pass, you must select "Start Battle Pass". If you don't see 'Start' button, then your BP has started.
  2. Progress on the Battle Pass by earning ARP from any source. The More ARP you earn, the farther you progress.
  3. After you complete a milestone, remember to claim the reward. (Don't worry, though. If you forget, all earned rewards will be distributed to you at the end of the Season.)
  4. ARP Boost on the Battle Pass gives you more ARP and progresses you further on the pass.
  5. You may spend your Battle Tokens when the Battle Pass concludes for a limited time.
  6. Battle Tokens are not retained between Seasons.
  7. Last rule: Have fun and always remember that everything on AWA will always be free!
